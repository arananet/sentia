# Reference boards: the production quality anchor

The two boards in this folder are the quality anchor for Sentia product
imagery. The product owner supplied them on 2026-10-30 as the standard
that every product image for Sentia is measured against.

## What the boards anchor

- Photorealistic studio product photography: real materials, real light,
  real skin. The patch reads as a physical object a camera photographed,
  never as a stylized or toy-like render.
- The exploded view shows each layer with believable materials: a matte
  silicone top cover, porous comfort foam, a detailed PCB with real
  components, and translucent adhesive and release liner films.
- On-skin shots look like skin and product in the same photograph:
  natural skin texture, a soft contact shadow, and the patch sitting
  into the surface it is applied to.
- Studio light and shadow are physically consistent across every view:
  one light logic, soft falloff, no pasted-on look.

## What a reviewer checks in any new product image

- Materials read as what they are: silicone, foam, PCB, adhesive film,
  skin.
- Skin contact looks believable: the patch rests on the skin with a
  contact shadow and a slight surface response, not floating above it.
- Light and shadow stay physically consistent with the anchor boards.
- Detail holds up at close range: fine texture on the cover, real
  component detail on the board, clean edges.

## Scope of the anchor

The boards carry labels and marketing text because they are reference
material. Website assets themselves stay free of text and labels. The
anchor governs realism and finish, not layout or copy.

## The identity anchor: product-reference.png

product-reference.png is the hero image of the square product, added on
2026-10-30 for work order wo-gh-8. The boards above anchor quality; this
file anchors identity. The product it shows is the canonical Sentia
Patch One: a flat, thin, matte off white square patch with strongly
rounded corners, worn directly on the skin with no carrier or frame
around it, the SENTIA wordmark in dark print, and a teal cyan LED line.

Every product image is compared against this reference for identity:
same matte finish, same direct skin mounting, same teal LED, same flat
proportions. An image whose LED glows amber, whose cover reads glossy,
which shows a fabric carrier frame, or whose patch reads thick or
domed depicts a different product and fails review, however good it
looks against the quality boards.
